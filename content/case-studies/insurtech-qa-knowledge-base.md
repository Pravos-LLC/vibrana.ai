## The situation

We'd worked with this insurtech for years — product sprints, real builds. So when
their leadership decided they needed an AI strategy, they called us instead of a
consultancy. They expected a strategy. We proposed something better.

Rather than hand over a deck, we ran the strategy session *in parallel with
building two working AI prototypes* — so the team could see, touch, and feel how
AI actually lands inside an organization: the workflows it changes, the roles it
reshapes, the way you build with it now.

So we went in. Two weeks on-site, in person. We interviewed their call-centre
people, sat behind them while they worked, listened in on live calls, and took a
full inventory of their systems.

## What we found

One shared inbox. A "knowledge base" that was a PDF of FAQs. And one very senior,
very capable agent spending his days running around answering everyone else's
questions — then, once a month, carving out time to monitor calls for quality and
compliance.

That was the bottleneck, and the opportunity. Automating it would do two things at
once: tighten governance, and give their most valuable person his time back — time
he could spend training a bench of young leaders who'd been starved of it while
the company scaled. The COO and the head of the call centre agreed. That became
the scope.

## What we built

Two prototypes, in two weeks, both grounded in what we'd watched them do.

- **Automated call monitoring & QA.** Every call is transcribed and scored against
  the criteria that matter — call quality *and* UK insurance compliance (FCA,
  data protection, Treating Customers Fairly, complaints handling). Breaches get
  flagged automatically; agents get specific coaching notes. Monthly spot-checks
  became continuous coverage.
- **A knowledge assistant.** We turned that PDF into a real, searchable knowledge
  base — 1,441 answers across every policy — so any agent can find the right
  answer in seconds, instead of shouting across the floor.

## What changed

- **Every call gets reviewed now** — not the 5% a human could get to once a month.
  Compliance risk stops hiding in the calls no one listened to.
- **Their senior expert got his month back.** Instead of QA and question-answering,
  he's coaching the next generation of team leads.
- **The strategy landed because they could feel it.** Two working prototypes told
  them more about their AI future than any slide could — and gave them a running
  start instead of a plan.
